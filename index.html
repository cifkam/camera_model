Photo tools

Interactive optics for photographers.

3D camera model

A view camera in 3D: standards with tilt, swing, rise and shift, the plane of sharp focus, depth of field, field of view, lens coverage and the Scheimpflug geometry, all live as you move the sliders.

2D camera model (side view)

The same camera seen from the side: lens tilt, the hinge and Scheimpflug points, the plane of sharp focus and the depth-of-field wedge, with the focus point and the hinge draggable. A Scheimpflug calculator you can see.

Calculators & tables

Depth of field with draggable charts, hyperfocal and depth-of-field tables, format equivalents, reciprocity failure and development time vs temperature.
